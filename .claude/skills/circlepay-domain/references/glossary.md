# CirclePay Glossary

- **Susu** — A rotating savings circle. A fixed group contributes a set amount each cycle; one member receives the whole pooled amount per cycle until all have been paid. Traditional Ghanaian saving method, digitised here.
- **Cycle** — One round of a Susu. Number of cycles equals the number of members. "Cycle 3 of 10" = third round of ten.
- **Payout order** — The sequence in which members receive the pot. Set at creation: **rotating** (fixed order) or **random** (drawn). "Your turn" marks the user's cycle.
- **Contribution** — Money paid **in** by a member (Susu) or a contributor (fundraiser). Collected from mobile money.
- **Payout** — Money paid **out**: the pot to the cycle's recipient (Susu) or the raised funds to the verified recipient/hospital (Medical).
- **Pool** — A concrete Susu group/instance (e.g. "Kumasi Traders Circle"). Has a status: `active`, `planning`, `completed`, an admin, members, monthly amount, and cycle length.
- **Fund** — Umbrella term for any money-organising entity. Types: **Susu**, **Medical**, **Education**, **Business**. Medical/Education/Business behave as goal-based fundraisers; Susu is rotating.
- **Beneficiary** — The person a fundraising (e.g. Medical) fund is for; may differ from the organizer.
- **Verified hospital** — A medical provider Moolre/CirclePay has verified; Medical payouts go **directly** to it. Shown with a "Verified hospital" badge.
- **Contributor / Donor** — Someone who gives to a fundraising fund. May be **Anonymous**.
- **Trust score** — A per-user, platform-wide measure of reliability (shown as 5 segments + a standing label like *Good standing*). Built from on-time payments and completed funds.
- **Defaulter** — A member who fails to contribute when due. Defaulting triggers a **platform-wide lock** (can't join other funds), not just removal from one circle.
- **Platform fee** — A small fee on a contribution (demo: GHS 5 on a GHS 500 contribution → GHS 505 total). CirclePay never holds the underlying savings.
- **MoMo** — Mobile Money. The primary rails (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) via Moolre.
- **USSD** — Phone menu codes (e.g. `*714#`, `*713*...#`, Moolre `*203#`) letting users transact without a smartphone/internet. ~55–60% of MoMo runs on USSD.
- **Ghana Card** — National ID; MoMo numbers are Ghana Card-verified, used for KYC/onboarding.
- **GHS** — Ghana Cedis, the currency. Displayed like "GHS 4,820.00".
- **Akwaaba** — "Welcome" (Twi); used in greetings.
